Most hunger has no finish line because nobody drew one. The target moves the moment you near it, and that feels like ambition, though often it is just drift. Drawing a line is not settling. It is deciding, ahead of time, what would count. Write it in plain terms: an amount, a schedule, a kind of week. Then keep reaching, if you want to. The difference is that you will know when you have arrived, and you will be able to feel it instead of sprinting past it. People who know their enough tend to take bigger swings, because the floor under them is visible. The line can move later. It just has to exist first.
