The things we have for a long time slowly turn invisible. The working body, the steady partner, the kitchen table, the friend who always answers. One way to see them again is to imagine, briefly and concretely, that one of them was never there. Not to frighten yourself. Just to measure it. Picture the week without it, hour by hour. Most people come back from this with a quieter kind of gladness, and often a sharper sense of what deserves their time. It pairs well with ambition. Knowing what you already hold makes it easier to reach for more without gripping.
