Many of the things we want, we learned to want by watching someone else want them. That is not a flaw. It is how people pick up most of their tastes. The trouble starts when a borrowed goal runs your calendar and you never checked whether it fits. A borrowed hunger tends to feel urgent and joyless. It spikes when you see someone else ahead, and it fades when no one is watching. Your own hunger is steadier. It is still there on a quiet Sunday. You do not need to drop the borrowed ones. Just label them honestly, and give your best hours to the ones that are actually yours.
