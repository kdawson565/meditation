If the point of a song were its last note, the best musicians would play fastest. Nobody listens that way, and nobody should work that way either. A career, a company, a craft: each one is mostly middle. The arrival you are aiming at will last an afternoon, and then it becomes the next middle. That is not a reason to aim lower. It is a reason to notice that you are already inside the thing you wanted. The meeting, the draft, the late fix are not the toll you pay to get somewhere. They are the somewhere. Reaching and enjoying the reach are not opposites. They are the same motion, done awake.
