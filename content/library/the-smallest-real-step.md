On foggy days the problem is rarely desire. It is size. The project looks like a wall, so the hand never reaches for the first brick. Shrink the step until it is almost embarrassing: open the file, write the first sentence, send the one email. A real step is small but not fake. It moves the actual thing, even a little. Once it is done, you are no longer someone who is stuck. You are someone who has started, which is a different person to be on a gray afternoon. Momentum is not a mood you wait for. It is a result you build, one modest brick at a time.
