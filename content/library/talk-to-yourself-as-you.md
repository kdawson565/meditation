The voice in your head usually says "I", and it usually panics. Try switching to your own name, or to "you". It sounds small. It changes the distance. When you speak to yourself the way you would speak to a friend, the critic tends to quiet down and the advice gets better. You can still be direct. A good coach is not soft; they are on your side. Write two sentences to yourself in the second person about whatever is pressing today. Notice what you say when you are standing a step outside the problem. That is often the advice you already knew you needed.
