Every win has a long tail of people behind it. The teacher who was patient once. The colleague who covered a gap. The stranger who wrote the tool you used this morning. Naming them is not modesty. It is accuracy. Ambitious people often skip this step because it feels like giving away credit. It does the opposite. Seeing the chain makes you steadier, because you stop believing that everything rests on you. It also makes you better at the next thing, because you know who to call. Pick one recent win and trace it backward three links. Then tell one of those people what they did.
