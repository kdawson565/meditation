When something blocks the work, there are two problems: the thing itself, and the story you tell about it. The second one is usually heavier. Try separating them. The thing is a fact: the deal slipped, the launch moved, the person said no. The story is a forecast: this always happens, I should have known, it is over. Facts can be worked with. Forecasts mostly just weigh. Once you have the fact alone, ask what it makes possible that was not possible yesterday. Sometimes the answer is small: a clearer no, a better question, a free afternoon. The obstacle does not have to be welcome to be useful. It only has to be looked at plainly.
