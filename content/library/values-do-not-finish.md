reflection_question: When this goal is done or dropped, what value will you still be practicing?

---
A goal can be reached, missed, or replaced. A value cannot be finished. You never complete honesty or craft; you only live them again today. That is why values hold when results swing. If the outcome went the wrong way this week, ask a different question: did I act the way I meant to act? If the answer is yes, something real was kept, even if the scoreboard disagrees. This is not a consolation prize. Over years, the way you work tends to matter more than any single result, and it is the one part that was always yours. Results move. Values hold.
