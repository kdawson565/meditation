Calm days can feel suspicious to ambitious people, as if peace means the engine is off. It does not. A river looks quiet in the middle and still carries the boat. Settled is a good place to choose from. The trouble is only staying there by default, because nothing pulls. If the harbor has felt comfortable for a while, ask what you would regret not attempting. Not finishing, just attempting. Then take the calm with you. You do not have to trade your peace for your hunger. The best seasons tend to have both: a steady keel, and the sail up.
