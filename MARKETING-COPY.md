# Marketing site copy changes (iamballast.com)

From a read of the live site on 2026-11-06 (Home, About, The ritual, Practices). Nothing here has been edited on the site. The app now leads with values: "Grow without losing your peace."

## Fix first (things that are wrong, not just older)

1. **Every "Download app" button links to `/`, and the footer says "App download coming soon."** The app is live at https://app.iamballast.com and installs from the browser. Point every Download / Join button there, and replace "coming soon" with "Open Ballast on your phone, then Add to Home Screen."
2. **/faq shows a "Welcome back / Log in" screen** instead of FAQ content.
3. **The login screen offers "Log in with Lovable."** Remove it. Ballast accounts live in the app, not on the marketing site; "Log in" and "Join" should both go to https://app.iamballast.com.
4. **Practices page says "Twelve" practices.** The app now has 21. Add a **Values** group (below).

## New section for Home (between "The idea" and "The practices")

**Four layers**

- **Values** — chosen by you, lived daily. The ballast: low and steady.
- **Goals** — your Sails. Raised, changed, sometimes dropped.
- **Growth + Peace** — what Ballast promises: speed with stability.
- **Dials** — Drive, Peace, Voice. How today actually felt.
